# Weekly Content Plan

## Goal

Turn the social strategy into a simple week-by-week publishing plan with clear topic titles.
Each week should support one main intent, one matching site cluster, and one set of reusable creative assets.

## Week 1: Trust And Legality

Focus: make the service feel clear, safe, and legal for foreign travelers.

- `Can foreigners legally drive a tuk tuk in Sri Lanka?`
- `What is the AAC permit and why do you need it?`
- `What is included in a self-drive tuk tuk rental?`
- `What happens in the first 30 minutes after pickup?`

Best formats:

- Reddit answer post
- Instagram FAQ carousel
- TikTok or YouTube Short
- Google Business Profile update

Site pages to support:

- Homepage
- Permit page
- Lesson page
- Insurance page

## Week 2: Price And Value

Focus: explain the cost difference between self-drive tuk tuks and other transport options.

- `Real cost of transport in Sri Lanka: bus vs taxi vs self-drive tuk tuk`
- `How much does a tuk tuk rental cost in Sri Lanka?`
- `What hidden costs should travelers expect?`
- `Is self-drive cheaper than hiring a driver?`

Best formats:

- Cost comparison infographic
- Carousel post
- Reddit resource post
- Facebook group reply

Site pages to support:

- Homepage
- Price page
- FAQ section

## Week 3: Destination Starts

Focus: show that each city page has a different travel purpose.

- `Negombo airport pickup: the best first-night handover point`
- `Kandy tuk tuk rental: the best base for central Sri Lanka`
- `Ella tuk tuk rental: scenic hill-country driving made simple`
- `Mirissa and Galle: the best south-coast starts for beach trips`

Best formats:

- Destination reel
- Route map carousel
- Short guide video
- Community post

Site pages to support:

- Negombo page
- Kandy page
- Ella page
- Mirissa page
- Galle page

## Week 4: Route Planning

Focus: help travelers understand how the trip flows after pickup.

- `Negombo to Kandy route guide`
- `Kandy to Ella route guide`
- `Ella to Mirissa route guide`
- `Galle to Colombo route guide`

Best formats:

- Route map graphic
- POV driving clip
- YouTube Short
- Blog or long caption post

Site pages to support:

- Route pages
- Location pages
- Homepage internal links

## Week 5: Beginner Confidence

Focus: reduce fear for first-time three-wheeler drivers.

- `How hard is it to drive a tuk tuk in Sri Lanka?`
- `The first 30 minutes as a new tuk tuk driver`
- `Common beginner mistakes to avoid`
- `How the gears and controls work`

Best formats:

- How-to carousel
- Short driving lesson video
- Reddit explanation post
- YouTube long form

Site pages to support:

- Lesson page
- FAQ page
- Homepage

## Week 6: Proof And Support

Focus: show how support works and why travelers can trust the service.

- `What does 24/7 roadside support actually mean?`
- `How permit support works before pickup`
- `What to do if your route changes`
- `Why foreign travelers choose SK Tuk Rides`

Best formats:

- Trust graphic
- Q&A post
- Behind-the-scenes reel
- Google Business Profile update

Site pages to support:

- Homepage
- Support page
- Insurance page
- Permit page

## Weekly Publishing Rule

- Publish one post title from the weekly list as the main post.
- Repurpose the same topic into one short video, one carousel, and one community reply.
- Use the matching site page as the only CTA destination.
- Keep the title wording close to the page title for better topical alignment.

# 30-Day Ranking Roadmap

## Goal

Improve indexation, topical relevance, and crawl trust for `promo.sktukrides.com` so the site can rank for long-tail tuk tuk rental queries first, then expand into broader terms.

## Week 1: Indexing And Crawl Control

- Submit `https://promo.sktukrides.com/sitemap.xml` in Search Console.
- Request indexing for the homepage and the five destination pages once.
- Confirm the live pages return `index, follow`, correct canonicals, one H1, and valid titles and descriptions.
- Keep the parked language pages `noindex`.
- Make sure the deploy artifact includes the `assets/` directory.
- Check Search Console `Pages` and `Sitemaps` reports for exclusion reasons.

### Social Content For Week 1

- Publish one brand intro post: what SK Tuk Rides is and who it is for.
- Publish one airport pickup post for Negombo.
- Publish one FAQ carousel: "Can foreigners legally drive a tuk tuk in Sri Lanka?"
- Publish one short video: airport handover, keys, and first-drive explanation.

## Week 2: Money Pages

- Publish the permit page.
- Publish the pricing page.
- Publish the driving lesson page.
- Publish the insurance page.
- Publish the foreigners page.
- Add strong internal links from the homepage to each of those pages.
- Add FAQ sections on each page.

### Social Content For Week 2

- Publish one permit explainer carousel.
- Publish one pricing breakdown graphic.
- Publish one "what is included" trust post.
- Publish one Reddit answer or post based on the permit question.

## Week 3: Route And Destination Authority

- Publish route pages for:
  - Negombo to Kandy
  - Kandy to Ella
  - Ella to Mirissa
  - Galle to Colombo
  - Colombo Airport to South Coast
- Add route links from the matching city pages.
- Improve destination copy so each city page speaks directly to that location.

### Social Content For Week 3

- Publish one route map carousel per major corridor.
- Publish one short driving tip video for hill country.
- Publish one short driving tip video for south coast roads.
- Publish one destination-specific post for each of Negombo, Kandy, Ella, Mirissa, and Galle.

## Week 4: CTR, Trust, And Links

- Review Search Console queries and page CTR.
- Rewrite titles and meta descriptions where impressions are high but clicks are weak.
- Add a real about page or business profile page if proof is available.
- Add real review proof only if the proof is visible on-page.
- Get a few relevant travel-community mentions or backlinks.
- Refresh the best-performing social posts and point them to the matching site pages.

### Social Content For Week 4

- Publish one comparison post: tuk tuk vs taxi vs car rental.
- Publish one beginner confidence post: first 30 minutes driving.
- Publish one safety/support post.
- Publish one summary post linking the top pages and guides.

## Social Content Matrix

| Platform | Best Content Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Reddit | Cost breakdowns, permit guides, route advice | Earn discussion and natural mentions |
| Facebook Groups | Helpful replies and local advice | Reach people already asking travel questions |
| Instagram | Carousels and route graphics | Shares and saves |
| TikTok | POV driving clips and short explainers | Discovery |
| YouTube Shorts | Same clips as TikTok, plus route tips | Extra discovery |
| YouTube Long Form | 3 to 6 minute explainers | Authority |
| Google Business Profile | Photos, Q&A, short updates | Trust and local proof |
| X | Short helpful replies | Secondary visibility |

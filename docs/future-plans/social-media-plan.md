# Social Media Plan

## Goal

Use social content to support search demand, branded discovery, and trust for the promo site.
Social posts do not replace SEO pages. They should reinforce the same topics, routes, and user questions that the site targets.

## Core Content Pillars

- Destination intent
- Permit and legality clarity
- Price and value explanation
- Beginner confidence
- Safety and support
- Route planning
- Real behind-the-scenes process

## What Social Content To Make

Use content that supports intent and trust. Not generic travel posts.

| Platform | Content To Make | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Reddit | Cost comparison post, AAC permit guide, first-time driver guide, route planning answer post | Earn discussion, traffic, and natural mentions |
| Facebook Groups | Helpful replies with route tips, airport pickup guidance, price clarifications | Reach travelers already asking questions |
| Instagram | Carousels for permit steps, cost breakdown, route maps, what is included | Saves and shares |
| TikTok | Short POV clips of driving, gear shifting, airport pickup, mountain roads | Discovery and branded search |
| YouTube Shorts | Same as TikTok, but focused on route and beginner clips | Extra discovery |
| YouTube long form | 3 to 6 minute explainers on renting, permit, lesson, safety, and route choice | Authority |
| Google Business Profile | Photo updates, Q&A, service posts | Trust and local credibility |
| X | Short helpful replies to travel questions, not promos | Secondary visibility |

## Posting Cadence

- 3 short-form videos per week
- 2 carousel posts per week
- 2 story sets per week
- 1 community post per week
- 1 long-form explanation post or video every 1 to 2 weeks

## Weekly Structure

### Monday

- Publish one trust or FAQ post.
- Use this for permit, safety, insurance, or legal clarity.

### Wednesday

- Publish one destination or route post.
- Use this for Negombo, Kandy, Ella, Mirissa, Galle, or a route page.

### Friday

- Publish one cost, comparison, or beginner-confidence post.
- Keep the message practical and useful.

### Weekend

- Publish one short video or story sequence from real vehicle, route, or handover footage.
- Repurpose the best-performing post into a second platform format.

## Content Creation Workflow

1. Pick one page or one topic.
2. Write one primary message.
3. Create one visual asset.
4. Add one CTA that points to the matching page.
5. Repurpose the same idea for one short video, one carousel, and one community reply.
6. Track the clicks and engagement from each post.

## How Each Social Post Should Be Built

- One post = one call to action.
- Use the same keyword phrase in the caption and the linked page title.
- Keep the first line useful, not salesy.
- Link only the matching page.
- Use real photos, screen recordings, maps, or simple graphics.

## What Matters Most For Ranking

- Internal links from homepage to location and support pages.
- Social posts that drive branded searches and mentions.
- Real external mentions from travel communities.
- Consistent page titles, H1s, and topic focus.
- Fast pages with visible proof instead of fake trust signals.

## Caption Rules

- Open with the search question or travel problem.
- Keep the first line specific.
- Mention only one URL.
- Use the same wording as the matching page title where possible.
- Do not invent testimonials or claims.

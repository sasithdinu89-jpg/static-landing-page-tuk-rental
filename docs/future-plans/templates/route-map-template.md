# Route Map Template

## Goal

Explain a specific route and why it works for foreign travelers using a simple map-led post.

## Working Title

`{{route_name}} route map for self-drive tuk tuk travelers`

## Hook

`This route is one of the easiest ways to see Sri Lanka by tuk tuk without wasting time on backtracking.`

## Structure

1. Name the start and end points.
2. Show the approximate drive time.
3. List the best stops on the route.
4. Mention road difficulty.
5. Add one practical driving tip.
6. End with the matching route or location page.

## Route Notes

- Start: `{{start_point}}`
- End: `{{end_point}}`
- Drive time: `{{drive_time}}`
- Best stops: `{{stops}}`
- Road type: `{{road_type}}`

## Suggested Caption

`{{One short paragraph explaining the route.}}`

`{{One short paragraph explaining why it helps foreign travelers.}}`

`Route guide: {{page_url}}`

## Visual Idea

- Route line graphic.
- 3 to 5 stop markers.
- One highlight box for the best photo stop.

## CTA

`See the full route guide and planning tips here: {{page_url}}`

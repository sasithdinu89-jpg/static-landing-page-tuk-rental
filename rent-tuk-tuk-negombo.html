Your Starting Point

Explore Negombo by Tuk Tuk

The perfect gateway city - minutes from the airport and packed with beaches, canals, and seafood.

Route 1: Coastal Strip

Drive the 15 km beach road from Negombo south to Uswetakeiyawa - golden sands, fishing villages, and fresh king prawn stalls en route.

Route 2: Dutch Canal Road

Follow the 17th-century Dutch Canal north to Chilaw through quiet backroads, lagoon views, and traditional fishing communities.

Route 3: Into the Wetlands

Head east to the Muthurajawela wetland reserve - a 30-minute tuk tuk ride to a spectacular mangrove ecosystem teeming with wildlife.

Local Tip: Pick up your tuk tuk the evening you land - Negombo is only 8 km from the airport. Start fresh the next morning and drive north at sunrise for the best coastal light.
Why Start in Negombo

Negombo Is the Practical First Stop for Foreign Travelers

Negombo works best when you want the least friction between landing in Sri Lanka and starting your self-drive trip.

Closest airport handover logic

Bandaranaike International Airport is the main arrival point for most foreign travelers, so Negombo keeps the first handover day simple.

Easy first-night transition

You can sleep near the airport, collect the tuk tuk, and start the road trip fresh without a long transfer inland.

Strong route flexibility

Negombo works for northbound coastal loops, direct drives toward Kandy, or full island circuits starting clockwise.

Best for first-time drivers

The flatter roads around Negombo make it a more forgiving place to settle into the vehicle before longer stages.
